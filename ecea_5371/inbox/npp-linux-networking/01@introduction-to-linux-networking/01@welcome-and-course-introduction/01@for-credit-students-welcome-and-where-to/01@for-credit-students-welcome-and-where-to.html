Welcome, students!

We are excited to have you with us here at the University of Colorado Boulder and we look forward to supporting you throughout your time with us! 

Course Content Support 

  1. Attend your Course Facilitator’s Office Hours

    1. You can access these via the Live Events tab on the left-hand side of the Course home page

  2. Reach out to your Course Facilitator via the course support email: msece-support@colorado.edu

    1. Please include the ECEA XXXX where XXXX is your course number in the subject line

  3. Ask your peers for support or suggestions in a discussion forum

    1. Note that Course Facilitators do not monitor discussion forums

Technical Support 

  1. Reach out to your Course Facilitator via the course support email: msece-support@colorado.edu

    1. Please include the ECEA XXXX where XXXX is your course number in the subject line

  2. Contact Coursera's technical support team

  3. Flag the issue in the course (non-urgent issues only)

    1. Course Facilitators check flags weekly, but an email to the course support email is preferred! 

Any other degree related support

  1. Please include the reason you are reaching out in the subject line

    1. Reach out to Program Staff via the course support email: msece-support@colorado.edu
