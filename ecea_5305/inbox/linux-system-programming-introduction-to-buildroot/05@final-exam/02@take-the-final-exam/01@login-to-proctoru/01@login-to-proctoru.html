Before you take the final exam for the course, you need to login to ProctorU. You can do that by going directly to the ProctorU website.

Once the exam process begins, your proctor will unlock the exam by completing the "Password Quiz" for you. 

If you have any issues during your proctored exam session, please reach out to msece-support@colorado.edu with the subject line ProctorU Assistance and we will be happy to assist you.
