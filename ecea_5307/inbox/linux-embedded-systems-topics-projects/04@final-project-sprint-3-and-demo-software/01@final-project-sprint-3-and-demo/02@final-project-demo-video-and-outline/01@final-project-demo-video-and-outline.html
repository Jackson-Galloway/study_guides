Each student in the project should create a wiki page "<Student Name> Final Project Video" in the shared project repo and share a link to this page as their assignment submission.

In multiple student group projects, each student should prepare their own video specific to their contribution.

See outline page template on GitHub.

  • The page should include an outline of the topics covered in the individual student demo video.

  • The outline should include challenges faced and lessons learned related to each student's contribution.

  • Please link your individual highlight video wiki pages from your Project Overview Wiki page in a new section "Student Video Demos".

  • Each student's individual demo video should provide an overview of the content which they were responsible for in the overall implementation.

  • Videos should be no longer than 5 minutes per student.

  • Each student should record a demo video, upload to google drive, youtube or other video sharing service, and share a public link to the video on their Video Demo page.

Please make the video publicly accessible with the link to support peer reviews.
