The course final examination is similar in format to the other graded assignments, with the following characteristics:

  • The exam is unproctored.

  • The exam is two hours long.

  • The exam is 34 questions.

  • You are allowed 1 attempt.

    • An attempt is one complete try at the exam within the given time limit.

  • Each attempt allows 1 submission.

    • A submission occurs when you click “Submit” during an attempt.

  • After the attempt window has ended, you will not be able to change or re-take the exam.

  • The exam contains multiple-choice questions and one free-response question. There are no programming questions.

  • You are not allowed to use any notes or access external websites during the exam.

You may drop or withdraw from this course at any time before accessing the final exam. Once you begin the exam, you will be assigned a course grade based on the announced grading formula, and this grade will appear on your university transcript after the eight-week session concludes. After the final exam has ended, you may continue submitting homework assignments until the conclusion of the eight-week session.
